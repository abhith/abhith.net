I'm Abhith Rajan, a [software engineer](https://github.com/abhith) from India who has been shipping production software since 2012 — the last decade of it in the UAE. Today I'm on the IT team at [National Air Cargo](https://www.nationalaircargo.com), working out of **Dubai Airport Freezone**.

I work across the whole stack. On the back end that's [.NET](/topics/dotnet/); on the front end it's [TypeScript](/topics/typescript/) with [React](/topics/react/), [Angular](/topics/angular/) or [Vue](/topics/vue/) — whichever fits the product and the team. Then I own the path to production: [Azure](/topics/azure/) infrastructure, [GitHub Actions](/topics/github-actions/) pipelines and the monitoring that tells us when something drifts. I care about clean architecture, boring deployments and code the next engineer can pick up without a tour.

Along the way the domains changed as much as the stacks: enterprise software in India, client products in Abu Dhabi, construction operations, a deep dive into fintech and credit data at [Emcredit](https://www.emcredit.com), and now air cargo.

This site is my public notebook — what I build, break and fix across .NET, Azure, the JavaScript ecosystem, DevOps and developer tooling — plus the snippets and links I keep coming back to.

You can find me on [X](https://x.com/abhithrajan), [GitHub](https://github.com/Abhith) and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/abhith/), or [say hello](/contact/) — email works too: [abhith@pm.me](mailto:abhith@pm.me).
